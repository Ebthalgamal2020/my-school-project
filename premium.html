Five things every family should know about us

One school, one path

From first steps to Abitur

Kindergarten

Ages 4–6

German arrives through play, song and routine — never through drilling. Children hear both languages every day until switching between them stops feeling like a switch.

  • Play-based bilingual immersion
  • Small groups, consistent teachers
  • School readiness in the final year

Grundschule

Grades 1–4 · Ages 6–10

Literacy in two scripts at once. Latin and Arabic letters are taught side by side, patiently, because reading in two directions is a genuine cognitive feat.

  • German, Arabic and English foundations
  • Mathematics on the German curriculum
  • Music, art and sport every week

Gymnasium

Grades 5–12 · Sek. I & II

Academic depth arrives here: sciences, humanities and languages taught to the German secondary standard, with the Abitur held steadily in view.

  • Full German secondary curriculum
  • Language certification pathways
  • Student council with real authority

Abitur

Grades 12–13

The German university-entrance qualification, recognised in Germany, Egypt and far beyond — earned in two languages rather than translated into one.

  • Recognised for German universities
  • University and careers guidance
  • An alumni network across two countries

Who teaches here

Teachers who stay long enough to know your child's name — and use it.

Our faculty mixes German-trained educators with long-standing Egyptian staff, many teaching here for more than a decade. That continuity is deliberate: children learn better from people who remember them from one year to the next.
