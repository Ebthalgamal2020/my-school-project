Our Heritage

Teaching in Cairo since 1873.

1873 Founded

The Deutsche Evangelische Oberschule Kairo has been part of this city since 1873. More than 150 years on, it still does the thing it was founded to do: teach a German curriculum inside Egyptian life, rather than beside it.

That continuity is the point. A school measured in generations can afford to think in decades — about how children learn to read in two scripts, how a timetable makes room for music and sport, and how a community keeps its character while the city around it changes.

Today the school teaches every stage from Kindergarten through to the Abitur, in German, Arabic and English.

Our Vision

Five commitments that shape the school.

These are the five pillars of the school's Leitbild. The German headings are the school's own; the English lines beneath are plain-language summaries.

01Begegnung

Encounter. German and Egyptian students share the same classroom by design, not by coincidence.

02Bildung

Formation. More than instruction — an interest in what a student becomes, not only what they can recall.

03Religion & Spiritualität

A Protestant foundation, open to every student, with room to ask difficult questions.

04Individuum & Gemeinschaft

The individual and the community. Space to become yourself inside a group that notices when you are missing.

05Partizipation

Participation. Students, parents and teachers each hold a real part in how the school runs.
