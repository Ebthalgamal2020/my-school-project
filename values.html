Our Values · DEO Kairo

What connects us
moves us forward.

Five values, drawn as one line. Two separate strands — a German school and an Egyptian city — travel down this page and, value by value, become the single shape our mark has carried since 1873.

Scroll to begin
01

Connection

Germany and Egypt. Students and teachers. Past and future. Every day here begins with two traditions choosing to share one room rather than politely dividing it.

Encounter is a method here, not a motto.
02

Curiosity

A good question is worth more than a correct answer given too quickly. Lessons are built to leave room for the second question — the one nobody planned for.

We would rather a class ran long than ended early.
03

Community

Learning here is not a solo performance. Classes are small enough that an empty chair gets noticed, and mixed enough that nobody spends five years only among people like themselves.

A school is a community before it is a curriculum.
04

Creativity

Room to think sideways: a stage, a workshop, a rehearsal room, and teachers who treat an unexpected idea as progress rather than as a detour from the plan.

The best answers rarely arrive in the expected order.
05

Future

The Abitur is a door, not a destination. Students leave here at ease in three languages and at home in more than one country — which is a harder thing to teach than either.

Prepared for what comes next — wherever next turns out to be.
