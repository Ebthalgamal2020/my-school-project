Community · DEO Kairo

A school is made of people.

Not a building. Not a curriculum. Four groups of people who keep showing up for each other — and, by the end of this page, one shape they make together.

01 — Students

Energy, discovery, friendship.

Break-time is where the two halves of this school actually mix. Not because a timetable says so — because a football match does not care which language it is played in.

02 — Teachers

Guidance that outlasts a school year.

We do not teach a subject to a room. We teach the twenty-two people in it — and we are still here when they come back.

— A teacher at DEO Kairo. Illustrative quote written for this concept.

03 — Families

Connection beyond the classroom.

Two halves of one day: the hours here, and the hours at home. Neither works without the other.

04 — Alumni

The connection keeps travelling.

Leaving is not the end of the relationship. It is the point at which the school stops being a place you go to and starts being a place you are from.

Together

Three hundred and twenty people, one shape.

Every dot is a person. Scattered, they are a crowd. Gathered, they are the mark that has stood over this school since 1873 — and the pointer still moves them, because a community is never quite still.

Come and meet them in person.
