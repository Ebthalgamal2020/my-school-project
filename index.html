Life beyond the timetable

A school day that doesn't end when the bell does.

Sport, music, theatre and theme days aren't extras bolted onto the timetable — they're where friendships across two cultures actually get made. A pitch and a stage teach things a classroom cannot.

Every pupil joins at least one club. Teams train on campus, perform on campus, and travel together — because a school is a community before it is a curriculum.

Who teaches here

Teachers who stay long enough to know your child's name — and use it.

Our faculty mixes German-trained educators with long-standing local staff, many teaching here for over a decade. That continuity is deliberate: children learn better from people who remember them from one year to the next.
