School Path · Version 2

Take the century by hand.

Drag the dial, or arrow along it. As you move forward the archive wipes off the photograph — the school arrives in colour exactly as fast as you let it.

01

A few rooms, and a decision.

A German school opens in Cairo — and decides, from the start, that it will teach Cairo's children too. Everything after this follows from that one sentence.

02

Two traditions learn one timetable.

German rigour and Egyptian daily life stop being neighbours and start being colleagues. Neither is the guest of the other.

03

The school becomes a habit passed down.

Former pupils bring their own children through the same gate, and the corridors fill with names the staff already know.

04

One campus, three languages, one Abitur.

German, Arabic and English are the three languages a corridor conversation moves between without anyone noticing they have switched.

05

The road does not stop at the gate.

It loops. Alumni come back as parents, as teachers, as the people who decide what the next hundred and fifty years look like.
