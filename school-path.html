01 — The beginning

1873

A few rooms, and a decision.

A German school opens in Cairo — and decides, from the start, that it will teach Cairo's children too. Everything on this page follows from that one sentence.

  • Deutsche Evangelische Oberschule
  • Cairo

02 — A German-Egyptian connection

1900s

Two traditions learn one timetable.

German rigour and Egyptian daily life stop being neighbours and start being colleagues. Neither is the guest of the other — that is the difference between a foreign school and an encounter school.

  • Two languages
  • One campus

03 — Growing together

1970s

Siblings follow siblings.

The school stops being a building and becomes a habit passed down. Former pupils bring their own children through the same gate, and the corridors fill with names the staff already know.

  • Generations
  • Kindergarten to Abitur

04 — DEO today

Today

One campus in Dokki, three languages, one Abitur.

German, Arabic and English are not three subjects on a list — they are the three languages a corridor conversation moves between without anyone noticing they have switched.

  • Dokki, 12612 Cairo
  • KG to Grade 13

05 — The future

Tomorrow

The road does not stop at the gate.

It loops. Alumni come back as parents, as teachers, as the people who decide what the next hundred and fifty years look like — which is why our mark has no end point.
